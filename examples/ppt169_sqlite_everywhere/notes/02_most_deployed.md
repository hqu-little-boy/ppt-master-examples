Start with the claim that sounds like marketing: SQLite is likely used more than all other database engines combined. The evidence is a list of hosts. It ships in every Android device, every iPhone and iOS device, every Mac, every Windows 10 and 11 installation, and in Firefox, Chrome, and Safari. It is inside every copy of Skype, iTunes, the Dropbox client, TurboTax and QuickBooks; it comes with PHP and Python; and it runs in most television sets, set-top boxes, and car multimedia systems. Now the arithmetic: there are more than four billion smartphones in active use, each holding hundreds of SQLite database files, so there are probably over one trillion SQLite databases in use right now. The project's own best guess is that it is the second most deployed library after zlib, and because it is often statically linked many times per device, it might even be the first.
